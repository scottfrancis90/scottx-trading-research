Why they move opposite

The two pairs share one currency, but on opposite sides of the quote.

EURAUD - AUD is the 2nd currency
AUDCHF - AUD is the 1st currency

When AUD strengthens, EURAUD falls and AUDCHF rises. The stronger the shared currency's own moves (USD, EUR, AUD, NZD, CHF), the closer the mirror.

Groups mirror better than single pairs because averaging two pairs cancels the currencies that are not shared.

How to use it

  • Confirmation: EURUSD breaking up should come with USDCHF breaking down. If the mirror pair does not follow, the move is weaker.
  • Risk: long EURUSD + short USDCHF is nearly the SAME trade twice - count it as double size, not as two positions.
  • Not a free hedge: buying both sides mostly cancels out while you pay two spreads and two swaps.
  • Relationships drift: GBPUSD vs CADCHF went from 0.00 to -0.31 in 2024-26. Re-run fx_opposite.py every few months.
